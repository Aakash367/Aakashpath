Overview

Onwardpath Technology Solutions LLC ("Onwardpath," "we," "us," or "our") is a Green Bay, Wisconsin-based enterprise IT consulting and technical talent firm. We operate onwardpath.com and related web properties (collectively, the "Site").

This Privacy Statement explains what information we collect when you visit our Site, how we use it, and your rights with respect to that information. We are committed to handling information with transparency and care.

By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Statement. If you have questions, please contact us at info@onwardpath.com.

02

Information We Collect

We collect two categories of information through the Site: information you provide to us directly, and information collected automatically when you visit.

Information you provide directly

When you submit a contact form, request a consultation, or otherwise reach out to us, we may collect your name, work email address, organization name, job title, and the content of your message. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and manage our business relationship.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, we automatically collect certain non-personal information about your session, including:

  • Referral source and the URL path of pages you visit
  • Device type and general browser information
  • Geographic region (state or metropolitan area — not precise location)
  • Time and date of your visit
  • On-page behavior signals such as scroll depth and interaction patterns
  • Role or intent selections you make through our website's persona prompt, if you choose to interact with it
  • Session identifiers used to maintain continuity of your experience across pages

We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information automatically. Automatic collection occurs only when you visit the Site.

03

Website Personalization

The onwardpath.com website uses Perzo — our own anonymous personalization product — to tailor the content you see based on contextual signals from your session. This allows us to present the most relevant information to different types of visitors without requiring you to log in or identify yourself.

How Perzo works on this site: Perzo reads anonymous signals from your session — such as how you arrived, what page you landed on, your device type, and your geographic region — and uses these to match you to a pre-configured content Segment. If you interact with our role-selection prompt, your declared selection (a Persona) takes priority over any inferred signals. Neither Segments nor Personas require or collect personally identifiable information.

Segments — system-applied

Perzo evaluates contextual signals on each page load and automatically assigns your anonymous session to a Segment. For example, a visitor arriving from a healthcare industry referral may see content emphasizing our healthcare consulting practice, while a visitor arriving from a technology partner directory may see content more relevant to enterprise platform work. Segment matching is automated, operates entirely on anonymous signals, and requires no action on your part.

Personas — visitor declared

In some cases, you may see a lightweight prompt asking you to identify your interest or role — for example, whether you are a prospective consulting client, a technology leader evaluating Perzo, or a practitioner interested in career opportunities. If you make a selection, that declared intent is used to further tailor the content you see. Your selection may persist across pages during your visit; persistence across separate visits is subject to your browser's cookie settings.

No personal data required: Perzo's personalization operates entirely without PII. We do not tie segment or persona data to your name, email, or any identifier that could be used to identify you as an individual. You can clear any stored persona selection at any time by clearing cookies for onwardpath.com in your browser settings.

06

Cookies & Tracking

The Site uses cookies and similar browser storage mechanisms for the following purposes:

  • Session personalization: Perzo uses session-scoped storage to maintain your segment assignment and any persona selection across pages during your visit. These do not contain personally identifiable information.
  • Cross-visit persistence: If you have made a persona selection, that selection may be stored in a cookie so the Site can recognize your preference on return visits, subject to your browser's cookie settings.
  • Analytics: We may use standard analytics tools to understand aggregate traffic patterns, page performance, and content engagement. These tools collect non-personal, aggregated data.

You may configure your browser to decline or delete cookies at any time. Doing so will clear any persona selections and may affect how content is presented to you on return visits, but will not prevent you from accessing any part of the Site.

No third-party advertising cookies: We do not use third-party advertising networks, retargeting pixels, or cross-site behavioral tracking technologies on onwardpath.com.
