Our story

Built from delivery.
Defined by depth.

Fourteen years. Two enterprise platforms. One consistent focus - helping complex organizations build digital experiences that actually work.

2011
Founding

Founded in Green Bay, Wisconsin.

We started with a single conviction: enterprises deserved a consulting partner with genuine platform depth, not generalist delivery. Oracle WebCenter - the backbone of intranets, portals, and content management at Fortune 500 organizations was our focus from day one. Every engagement deepened that expertise.

2014
Global delivery

Delivery center opened in Chennai, Tamil Nadu.

This was a capability decision, not a cost one. Enterprise programs demand depth on both sides of the delivery chain. Senior practitioners who understand the platform, the client context, and the stakes involved. The Chennai center made that possible at scale, and the onshore/offshore model we built here still runs every major engagement today.

2016
Partnership

Oracle Gold Partner status achieved.

Five years of WebCenter implementations, migrations, and integrations recognized by Oracle at the Gold Partner tier. The designation isn't granted for tenure. It reflects verified delivery competency, certified expertise, and documented outcomes across complex enterprise programs. For our clients, it meant independent confirmation of what they already knew from working with us.

2018
Healthcare focus

Healthcare deepens. Oracle COLLABORATE.

Major engagements with national health insurers and academic medical centers shaped a specialization that went beyond vertical focus. It became a delivery posture. Healthcare organizations operate under real constraints: compliance, data governance, member-facing accountability. Our work in this period was where those constraints stopped being learning curves and became institutional knowledge. Exhibiting at Oracle COLLABORATE in Las Vegas put our WebCenter practice in front of the enterprise audience that mattered most and reinforced our standing in the Oracle partner community.

Alongside client work, we remained active in the Green Bay business community, including the Chamber of Commerce Golf Fundraiser. A Midwest firm, rooted in the region it serves.

2022
AEM practice

AEM practice established. Adobe Solution Partner.

When healthcare organizations began migrating from Oracle WebCenter to Adobe Experience Manager, we were already there. Same team, same clients, same delivery discipline applied to a new platform. The AEM practice wasn't a strategic pivot; it was the natural extension of a decade of enterprise content management expertise. Recognized as an Adobe Solution Partner, we now hold deep certified capability across both platforms. A combination few firms can offer.

Perzo launched.

Fourteen years of healthcare portal delivery made one problem impossible to ignore: sixty-two percent of member portal visits begin unauthenticated, with zero personalization. Every session that fails to self-serve and escalates to a live agent costs $5–$12. Perzo closes that gap with anonymous member personalization: intent detection, segment matching, and purpose-built experiences delivered before login. No PII. No infrastructure changes. A 90-day structured pilot to measurable deflection data.

Perzo isn't a new business idea. It is the product our delivery experience made inevitable — and a model that extends to any enterprise with authenticated digital touchpoints: financial services, retail, utilities, and beyond.

What We Believe

The principles that shape how we work

Lead with solutions, not capabilities

We don't walk into a room with a slide deck about our technology stack. We walk in with a point of view about the problem you're trying to solve and a defensible approach to solving it.

Respect every perspective in the room

The best outcomes come from teams where business stakeholders, technologists, and content authors all have a voice. We design engagements that make space for that.

Build to transfer, not to depend

Every engagement includes structured knowledge transfer. Your team should be more capable at the end than at the start. We measure success by how little you need us next time.

Regulated-industry fluency is non-negotiable

Compliance constraints, content review cycles, regulatory caution, multi-brand governance. We don't treat these as surprises. We plan for them because we've operated inside them — across healthcare, financial services, and energy.

Flat culture, deep ownership

We don't have layers of management between the people doing the work and the people making decisions. Every practitioner on our team owns their deliverables and has a direct line to leadership.

Earn the next conversation

We don't assume we'll be invited back. Every engagement, every deliverable, every interaction is an opportunity to demonstrate that we deserve the next one.
